# AI Presentations related to Education

Short presentations and discussion materials about artificial intelligence in education.

## Slide deck sources

- [AI in Education Workshop](ai_introduction/ai-workshop-slides.qmd) - Frames AI use around volition and mental effort, then leads faculty through a collaborative problem-set activity.
- [AI Codex Setup](ai_codex/ai-codex-setup-slides.qmd) - Walks BYU-Idaho departments through signing in, installing Codex, connecting it to VS Code, and trying a small teaching-tool activity.
- [AI and Statistics Education](ai_stats_leaders/ai-stats-leaders-slides.qmd) - Highlights research on mental models, novice and expert use of GenAI, deliberate practice, assessment, and the path from novice to expert.
- [Why AI Works](why_ai_works/why-ai-works-slides.qmd) - Explains large language models through nonlinear dynamics, scaling laws, emergent abilities, grokking, criticality, in-context learning, and the manifold hypothesis.

The rendered presentations are linked from the [project presentation index](index.md).
